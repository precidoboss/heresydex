Bridge
The Grotto L1 ⇄ Avalanche C-Chain, with an optional auto-swap on arrival via GrottoBridgeHub.
🌉
Routed through GrottoBridgeHub on Grotto and AvalancheBridgeReceiver on Avalanche. You can send any HERESY DEX token — the hub swaps it into BOB, CAVE or USDC on Grotto if needed, bridges that, then the receiver auto-swaps into whatever token you pick on Avalanche via the Avalanche HeresyRouter. If that final swap can't fill your minimum (bad quote, no liquidity, etc.), the receiver contract catches it and simply forwards you the raw bridged asset instead — funds are never stuck, worst case you just don't get the auto-swap. Test with a small amount first.

🌉
This sends a real send() transaction to the ERC20TokenHome contract, using the standard ICTT ABI and a destination blockchain ID read live from Avalanche's Warp precompile (not hardcoded). Built from the public ICTT spec plus your own successful bridge tx trace, but not pulled from the official frontend's source — test with a small amount first. If anything looks off, use Open Official Bridge instead.
